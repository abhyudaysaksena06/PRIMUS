# PRIMUS for Finance funds, explained simply

**You manage an investment fund** with $500,000. Your score is how much you grow **all the money you were given.**

## How the night goes

1. **Round 1 · Practice, then pick winners (21:10–22:45).** While the PRDs are written, trade the **practice
   market** (Trade → Practice): $100,000 of practice money on live candlestick charts of simulated companies (one
   for every Finance team that checked in), a new price every 5 seconds and a candle every 30. Patterns are marked
   on the chart (▲ bullish, ▼ bearish) with what they usually mean. The practice money stays there, but the 3 funds
   worth the most at the close (22:00) each win $50,000 in real cash before the IPO (you must make at least one
   trade). Then read every company's PRD, ask questions on the Q&A board, and bid for shares in the **IPO**.
2. **Round 2 · Trade the crises (22:45–04:45).** Four crises hit every company; each one knocks prices down 5%.
   Then each company's response plan is scored, and the score moves its price. Trade in every trading round:
   **BUY** if you think a price will rise, **SELL** what you own, **SHORT** if you think it will fall, **COVER**
   to close a short. A company can also **sell 1–10% of itself** to a fund at the market price: you may fund one
   such raise per crisis, and hold at most 10,000 shares of any one company through raises.
3. **Round 3 · Fresh capital (04:45–06:30).** You receive half your starting money again. Round 3 evens
   out the field: a good solution lifts the weakest companies most (×1.25 of the rise) and the leaders least (×0.25),
   ranked by their average score so far; a bad solution's fall applies in full.

## Tips

- The order screen shows each company's candles beside the ticket: one candle per trading round (with its real high
  and low), the IPO, crises and score moves. While a round is open, its live candle shows the price the round
  **would clear at** with the orders placed so far.
- A company whose last round moved the full 10% takes **no orders in the next round** (a one-round cooling-off);
  the trade desk lists them.
- The **sector race** on the Market page and on the big screen shows which sector is rising.
- The **Leaderboard** shows every company's **PRIMUS Rating**: it starts at 1,000 and moves with each released
  PRD, crisis plan and solution score, compared with the other companies of the same sector (at most ±100 a round).
- Who hired which firm, and for how much, is public in the crisis room: strong firms may mean strong plans.
- You can't trade your own squad's company.
- **One screen per team.** Extra phones or laptops still work, but they update more slowly.
