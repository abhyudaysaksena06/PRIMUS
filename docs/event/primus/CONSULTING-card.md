# PRIMUS · Consulting firm card (C01–C200)

**You are a consulting firm.** Companies hire you for advice and pay you fees.
**Final value = cash (fees and bonuses) + fee shares × the closing price.** Tie-break: your average client rating.

| When | Do | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 21:10–22:00 | **Crisis memo** (Round 1) | The same case for every firm (ZestUp), up to 500 words, 6 sections. Bonus: $500 × (score − 50), from −$10,000 to +$25,000. The score also sets your **base rate**: 2% + 0.04% per point above 50 (or − per point below), between 1% and 4% of the client company. |
| 22:00–22:30 | **Call 1**: BUY or SELL on the 2 companies you follow | IPO price against the price after trading round 4 (about 23:50). Each correct call earns $2,500. |
| any time | **Fill in your firm's profile** | Headline, specialities, rate card, asking fee. Companies choose from it. |
| first 20 min of each crisis | **Answer offers** | Offers start at your base rate (your minimum bid). Accept, decline or counter (3 counters each). At most 2 clients per crisis. Accepting is final. A company that hires no one gets a random firm with room at that firm's base rate: you may be assigned a client. |
| by +65 min | **Advice memo for each client** | **150 to 500 words.** No memo, or under 150 words = **no fee** (it goes back to the client). |
| after the scores | **Bonus** | $500 × (memo score − 50) (from −$10,000 to +$25,000) + $1,000 × (stars − 3), for each client whose fee you earned. No rating = 3 stars. |
| from the end of the draft (about 04:50) to 06:00 | **Round 3** | Your client gets its statement in the draft (the 10 lowest-scoring companies in each sector pick, each statement once; every other company is given one at random, spread evenly across the ten). Get hired by 05:15, write your memo and help your client's solution (you can edit it with them). |
| 07:10 | Awards | |

**Tips**
- A complete profile wins more offers. Ratings are public and stay on your profile.
- Write each memo for that client's own crisis card and numbers; read their plan as it grows.
- Use the hire chat to agree with your client what they need.
- **Type everything**: paste is off. Drafts save themselves; Submit opens in the last 5 minutes and the last saved draft is submitted at the deadline. Over 500 words = not submitted.
- **One screen per team.** The **Leaderboard** shows each company's PRIMUS Rating: a quick read on your clients.

**Memo sections**: Diagnosis · Options · Recommendation · Numbers · Risks · Next steps.
**Advice sections**: Diagnosis · What to do · Evidence and numbers · Why it fits your client.
