# PRIMUS · Finance fund card (F01–F200)

**You are an investment fund with $500,000.** At 04:45 you receive $250,000 more.
**Score = your return on all the money you were given.** Tie-break: a smaller largest single position.
You may never trade your own squad's company.

| When | Do | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 21:10–22:00 | **Practice market** (Trade → Practice) | $100,000 of practice money on 30-second candlestick charts of simulated companies (one per checked-in Finance team). The practice money stays there, but the **3 funds worth the most win $50,000 each** in real cash before the IPO. Trade at least once to be ranked. |
| 22:00–22:30 | **Read the PRDs, ask questions** | The pitch book and the Q&A board. |
| 22:30–22:45 | **IPO bids** | Up to 4,000 shares per company, at its IPO price. Not your own squad's company. Oversubscribed: shares shared out pro rata (to 10 shares). |
| 22:45–06:30 | **Trade** in each trading round | BUY, SELL, SHORT or COVER. Every order fills at the round's new price. Long cap 4,000 shares; short cap 2,000; shorts lock 150% collateral; short exposure at most $250,000. A company whose last round moved the full 10% takes no orders in the next round. |
| Round 2 | **Fund a raise** (optional) | A company sells 1–10% of itself at the market price. One raise per fund per crisis; at most 10,000 shares of one company through raises. |
| each crisis | **Read the cards** | Every price falls 5% at the reveal; each company's plan score then moves its price. The last round of a crisis waits for those scores. |
| 04:45 | **Fresh capital** | +50% of your starting money. Round 3 evens out the field: price rises are ×1.25 for the weakest company down to ×0.25 for the strongest (by score so far); falls apply in full. |
| 06:30 | Market closes | Every short is covered at the closing price. |
| 07:10 | Awards | |

**Tips**
- Read the candles: ▲ bullish and ▼ bearish patterns (hammer, engulfing, breakout…) are marked on every chart.
- While a round is open, the chart shows the price it **would clear at** with the orders so far.
- The Market page shows the **sector race**: which sector's companies are rising since the IPO.
- The **Leaderboard** shows each company's PRIMUS Rating (starts at 1,000; moves with each released score against its sector, at most ±100 a round).
- Who hired which firm, and for how much, is public in the crisis room.
- Spread your bets: your largest single position decides a tie.
- **One screen per team**: extra devices update more slowly.
