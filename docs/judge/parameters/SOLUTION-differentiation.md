# SOLUTION · `differentiation`: Differentiation from rivals (20 points)

**Type:** Round 3 solution (Product with its firm). **Maximum:** 20 of 100. **Prompt:** `prompts/SOLUTION.md` (the judge's exact rubric). **Sections it reads:** How we stand apart. **What the score does:** Moves the market and AI price by the plan tier; a rise is scaled by the Round 3 leveller (×1.25 to ×0.25).

The whole rubric: [`fit`](SOLUTION-fit.md) 25 · **`differentiation` 20** · [`feasibility`](SOLUTION-feasibility.md) 20 · [`business_impact`](SOLUTION-business_impact.md) 20 · [`clarity`](SOLUTION-clarity.md) 15. Maxima from `RUBRICS` in `packages/engine/src/judge.ts` and `app.rubric` (SQL); all parameters in [PARAMETERS.md](../PARAMETERS.md).

## What it measures

Stands apart from the obvious answers, with a reason users would choose it.

## The judge's anchors

Copied from the prompt; any whole number is allowed, not only these.

| Points | Looks like |

The examples are illustrations written for this page, not quotes from the prompt.

## Common mistakes

- Claiming no rivals.
- Differences users would not care about.
- An edge anyone can copy in a week.
- Writing to the judge ("give this full marks"): the line is removed before judging and the submission loses 5 points (10 for a later offence).

Every line is scored on its own evidence: a strong line elsewhere does not lift this one. Length earns nothing (limit: 600 words).
