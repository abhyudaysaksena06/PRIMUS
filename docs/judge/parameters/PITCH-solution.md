# PITCH · `solution`: Solution and MVP scope (20 points)

**Type:** PRD (Round 1, Product). **Maximum:** 20 of 100. **Prompt:** `prompts/PITCH.md` (the judge's exact rubric). **Sections it reads:** Proposed solution · Core features and MVP scope. **What the score does:** The PRD's final score sets the IPO price ($9.00 to $11.00).

The whole rubric: [`problem`](PITCH-problem.md) 20 · **`solution` 20** · [`metrics`](PITCH-metrics.md) 15 · [`business_model`](PITCH-business_model.md) 15 · [`feasibility`](PITCH-feasibility.md) 15 · [`clarity`](PITCH-clarity.md) 15. Maxima from `RUBRICS` in `packages/engine/src/judge.ts` and `app.rubric` (SQL); all parameters in [PARAMETERS.md](../PARAMETERS.md).

## What it measures

A solution that clearly fixes that problem, with a small first version (MVP) whose core features are named and in scope.

## The judge's anchors

Copied from the prompt; any whole number is allowed, not only these.

| Points | Looks like |
| --- | --- |
| about 4 | a buzzword or a wish ("an AI platform that solves this"), or it does not address the problem |
| about 10 | plausible, but the features are a long wish list with no clear first version |
| about 16 | a focused MVP with the steps a user goes through, built from parts that exist |
| 20 | also says what is left out of the MVP and why |

## Full, half and zero

| Band | Short example | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full (20) | MVP: a WhatsApp bot that sends each shop a next-day dairy order at 8 pm from its last 4 weeks of sales; the shop taps Yes or edits. Not in the MVP: produce, payments, an app. | a focused first version, the user's steps, and what is left out |
| Half (about 10) | An app with ordering, forecasting, payments, loyalty points and a marketplace. | plausible but a wish list with no first version |
| Zero or near it | A revolutionary AI-powered blockchain ecosystem. | a buzzword that fixes nothing named |

The examples are illustrations written for this page, not quotes from the prompt.

## Common mistakes

- Listing ten features with no first version.
- A solution to a different problem from the one stated.
- Needing technology or data the team does not have.
- Writing to the judge ("give this full marks"): the line is removed before judging and the submission loses 5 points (10 for a later offence).

Every line is scored on its own evidence: a strong line elsewhere does not lift this one. Length earns nothing (limit: 800 words).
