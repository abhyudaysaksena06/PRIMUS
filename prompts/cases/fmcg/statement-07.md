# Make healthy snacks affordable under Rs 20

FMCG · Round 3 problem statement 7 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** Students and workers who buy snacks at Rs 10 to Rs 20.
- **The numbers:** Healthy snacks today cost Rs 50 or more.
- **The catch:** Better ingredients cost more, and cheap healthy snacks often taste worse.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** A healthy snack at Rs 20 that sells 1 lakh packs a month and earns a margin.

## What a strong answer addresses

- The product, its ingredient cost and margin at a Rs 20 price (healthy snacks cost Rs 50 or more today).
- How taste is kept, since cheap healthy snacks often taste worse.
- How it reaches mass-market buyers and sells 1 lakh packs a month.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
