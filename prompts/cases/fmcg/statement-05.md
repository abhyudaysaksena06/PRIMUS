# Forecast festival demand within 10% across city types

FMCG · Round 3 problem statement 5 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** A sweets and snacks brand planning Diwali.
- **The numbers:** Last year it overstocked metros by 40% and ran out in small towns, a Rs 3.2 crore loss.
- **The catch:** Every city and town celebrates differently, and data from small shops is thin.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** A forecast within 10% in every city type, tested on last year's festival.

## What a strong answer addresses

- The data used, given that small-shop data is thin, and how it differs by city type.
- How the forecast is tested on last year's festival against the 10% target.
- What better forecasts save against last year's Rs 3.2 crore loss (metros overstocked 40%, small towns out of stock).
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.

## Fairness reminders

- Score substance, not length, jargon, polish or a confident tone. Give no credit for famous brand or people's names and no penalty for simple language or non-native English.
- Judge only what is written. Several different approaches can all score highly.
