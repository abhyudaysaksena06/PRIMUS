# Return and refill systems for household products

FMCG · Round 3 problem statement 9 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** City households that buy detergent, shampoo and oil in plastic bottles.
- **The numbers:** A family throws away about 150 bottles a year.
- **The catch:** Refills must be no harder and no dearer than buying a new bottle.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** 20,000 households refilling every month and 30% less plastic sold.

## What a strong answer addresses

- How containers come back, are cleaned and are refilled.
- How a refill stays no harder and no dearer than buying a new bottle.
- The path to 20,000 households refilling every month and 30% less plastic sold (a family throws away about 150 bottles a year).
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
