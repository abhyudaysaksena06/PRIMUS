# Plastic-free packaging that costs under Rs 1 more per pack

FMCG · Round 3 problem statement 3 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** A snacks brand selling 2 crore packs a month.
- **The numbers:** Plastic packaging costs Rs 1.20 a pack; the alternatives cost Rs 3 or more.
- **The catch:** Food must stay fresh for 6 months in hot, humid shops.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** Plastic-free packs at under Rs 2.20 a pack with the same shelf life.

## What a strong answer addresses

- The packaging approach, its cost per pack at 2 crore packs a month, and its supply.
- How it keeps food fresh for 6 months in hot, humid shops, and how that is tested.
- The cost against the target of under Rs 2.20 a pack (Rs 1.20 today).
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.

## Fairness reminders

- Score substance, not length, jargon, polish or a confident tone. Give no credit for famous brand or people's names and no penalty for simple language or non-native English.
- Judge only what is written. Several different approaches can all score highly.
- The rubric decides the score; this brief only gives context.
