# Win shelf space on quick-commerce apps against big brands

FMCG · Round 3 problem statement 6 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** A new healthy-food brand on quick-commerce apps.
- **The numbers:** The top 3 brands take 80% of the first screen, and paying for placement costs 25% of sales.
- **The catch:** The apps own the customer data and change their rankings often.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** First-screen placement in 3 cities while spending under 10% of sales on it.

## What a strong answer addresses

- How a small brand earns first-screen placement when the top 3 brands take 80% of it.
- What it offers the apps, given that they own the data and change rankings often.
- The cost of placement against the target of under 10% of sales (25% today), in 3 cities.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
