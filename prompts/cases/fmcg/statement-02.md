# Reach 50,000 rural kirana shops profitably

FMCG · Round 3 problem statement 2 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** A biscuit and soap company that sells in cities but hardly reaches villages.
- **The numbers:** A rural kirana orders Rs 2,000 a week, and each delivery costs Rs 300.
- **The catch:** Roads, cash payments and tiny orders make every visit expensive.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** 50,000 shops served at a profit on every order within 18 months.

## What a strong answer addresses

- The ordering, delivery and payment model for rural kiranas (orders of Rs 2,000 a week).
- The economics per order against the Rs 300 cost of a delivery, and how every order makes a profit.
- How it reaches 50,000 shops within 18 months, given roads, cash payments and tiny orders.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.

## Fairness reminders

- Score substance, not length, jargon, polish or a confident tone. Give no credit for famous brand or people's names and no penalty for simple language or non-native English.
- Judge only what is written. Several different approaches can all score highly.
- The rubric decides the score; this brief only gives context.
