# Cut fresh-food waste from 9% to 3% across the supply chain

FMCG · Round 3 problem statement 1 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** A dairy and fresh-produce brand that sells through 30,000 shops.
- **The numbers:** 9% of its fresh stock is thrown away, worth Rs 60 crore a year.
- **The catch:** Shops do not share their sales data and have no space for new equipment.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** Waste at 3% within a year, with shops earning more than before.

## What a strong answer addresses

- Where in the chain the 9% waste happens, and what the solution changes there.
- How it works given that shops do not share sales data and have no space for new equipment.
- The saving against the Rs 60 crore a year, and how shops end up earning more than before.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
