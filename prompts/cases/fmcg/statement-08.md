# Trace every ingredient from farm to pack in seconds

FMCG · Round 3 problem statement 8 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** A spice and tea company with 4,000 farmers.
- **The numbers:** Tracing a single ingredient today takes 3 weeks and a phone call to each supplier.
- **The catch:** Farmers have basic phones, and middlemen do not want to be seen.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** Every pack traceable to its farms in under 10 seconds.

## What a strong answer addresses

- How data is captured at each step for 4,000 farmers with basic phones.
- Who enters it, how it is verified, and how middlemen who do not want to be seen are handled.
- How a pack is traced in under 10 seconds (3 weeks today).
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.

## Fairness reminders

- Score substance, not length, jargon, polish or a confident tone. Give no credit for famous brand or people's names and no penalty for simple language or non-native English.
- Judge only what is written. Several different approaches can all score highly.
- The rubric decides the score; this brief only gives context.
