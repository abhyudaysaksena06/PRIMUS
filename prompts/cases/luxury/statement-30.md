# Circular luxury: repair, resell and refurbish at scale

Luxury · Round 3 problem statement 30 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** A luxury group with 50,000 old pieces in its clients' cupboards.
- **The numbers:** Each piece can be repaired and resold 2 or 3 times; today 5% are.
- **The catch:** Resale must not make new pieces look cheaper.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** Resale and repair worth 15% of the group's revenue within 3 years.

## What a strong answer addresses

- How pieces come back from clients' cupboards and are authenticated, restored and resold.
- How resale protects rather than cheapens the price of new pieces.
- The path from 5% of 50,000 pieces today to resale and repair worth 15% of revenue within 3 years.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.

## Fairness reminders

- Score substance, not length, jargon, polish or a confident tone. Give no credit for famous brand or people's names and no penalty for simple language or non-native English.
