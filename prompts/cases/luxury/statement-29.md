# Private, secure data for high-net-worth clients

Luxury · Round 3 problem statement 29 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** Families whose wealth, travel and purchases are tracked by apps, staff and hackers.
- **The numbers:** One leak can cost a client crores in fraud or kidnap risk.
- **The catch:** Clients want privacy and the convenience of apps at the same time.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** A service clients trust with their data, audited by an outside firm, that nobody leaves.

## What a strong answer addresses

- What data is protected and how clients keep app convenience.
- How leaks are prevented and handled, and how an outside audit is arranged.
- Why clients trust it and stay, and how that is measured.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
