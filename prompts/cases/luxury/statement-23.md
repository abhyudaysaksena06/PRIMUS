# Prove any luxury item is genuine in seconds

Luxury · Round 3 problem statement 23 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** Resale shops and buyers of second-hand luxury bags and watches.
- **The numbers:** 1 in 8 items offered for resale is fake, and an expert check takes 3 days and costs Rs 4,000.
- **The catch:** Counterfeits now pass the checks experts use, so a better eye is not enough.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** A check in under 60 seconds, at under Rs 200, that catches 99% of fakes.

## What a strong answer addresses

- How an item is checked at the point of sale, given that counterfeits now pass expert checks.
- What happens to items already in circulation without any record.
- The accuracy, time and cost it can promise against 99%, under 60 seconds and under Rs 200 (1 in 8 items is fake today).
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
