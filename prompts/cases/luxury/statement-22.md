# One personal assistant for a client's whole luxury life

Luxury · Round 3 problem statement 22 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** A family that spends on travel, fashion, art and cars through 20 separate concierges.
- **The numbers:** They spend about Rs 3 crore a year and lose roughly 40 hours a month to arranging it.
- **The catch:** They share their data with no one they do not trust completely.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** One assistant they use every week, and a fee they pay without asking for a discount.

## What a strong answer addresses

- Which needs the single assistant brings together across 20 concierges and Rs 3 crore of spending a year.
- How the family's data is kept private, since they share it only with those they fully trust.
- What the family pays and why they would use it every week (about 40 hours a month lost today).
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
