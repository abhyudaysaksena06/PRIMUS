# Fractional ownership of art, cars or watches

Luxury · Round 3 problem statement 24 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** Young professionals who earn well but cannot buy a Rs 50 lakh painting or car.
- **The numbers:** A share can start at Rs 25,000, and a year of ownership must cost less than renting the item.
- **The catch:** Owners argue about who uses the car, when to sell and who pays for insurance.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** Shares that sell again within a week at a fair price, with no disputes left open.

## What a strong answer addresses

- Which assets, how shares are priced and resold within a week, and how a year of ownership costs less than renting.
- Who looks after the asset, and how use, sale and insurance disputes are settled.
- Any legal or regulatory points that shares in an asset raise.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
