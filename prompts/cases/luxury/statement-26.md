# Turn a fashion brand into a hospitality experience

Luxury · Round 3 problem statement 26 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** A fashion house that wants clients to stay, eat and celebrate inside its world.
- **The numbers:** A hotel costs Rs 200 crore to build; the brand has Rs 20 crore to spend.
- **The catch:** Hospitality is a different business, and one bad stay is seen by the whole fashion world.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** A first experience that sells out and earns money within 12 months.

## What a strong answer addresses

- The experience chosen and how it fits the brand within Rs 20 crore (a hotel costs Rs 200 crore).
- How the risk of one bad stay is managed in an unfamiliar business.
- The economics per guest or member and the path to selling out and earning money within 12 months.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.

## Fairness reminders

- Score substance, not length, jargon, polish or a confident tone. Give no credit for famous brand or people's names and no penalty for simple language or non-native English.
- Judge only what is written. Several different approaches can all score highly.
- The rubric decides the score; this brief only gives context.
