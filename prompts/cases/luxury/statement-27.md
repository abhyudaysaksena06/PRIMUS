# Luxury materials that are fully traceable and sustainable

Luxury · Round 3 problem statement 27 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** Brands that use leather, gold and silk and are asked where they came from.
- **The numbers:** Under 20% of their materials can be traced to the source today.
- **The catch:** Suppliers guard their sources, and new materials must still feel luxurious.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** 80% of materials traced, with proof a client can see on the product.

## What a strong answer addresses

- Which materials, how each step is traced and proven, and how sources are checked independently.
- How suppliers who guard their sources are brought in, and how materials still feel luxurious.
- The premium or cost involved, and how '80% traced, with proof on the product' (under 20% today) is measured.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
