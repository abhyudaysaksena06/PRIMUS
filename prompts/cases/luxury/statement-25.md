# After-sales care as good as the purchase

Luxury · Round 3 problem statement 25 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** Clients who bought a Rs 5 lakh piece and waited 3 months for a repair.
- **The numbers:** Repairs take 90 days on average and 30% of clients do not buy again after one.
- **The catch:** The best craftsmen are few, and are in Europe.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** Repairs back in under 14 days and clients who buy again more often after a repair than before it.

## What a strong answer addresses

- How repair turnaround falls from 90 days to under 14, given that the best craftsmen are few and in Europe.
- How quality is controlled and how the client is kept informed.
- How the effect on repeat buying is measured (30% of clients do not buy again after a repair today).
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
