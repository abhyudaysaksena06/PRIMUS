# Win the next generation of luxury buyers

Luxury · Round 3 problem statement 28 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** Buyers aged 22 to 30 who prefer experiences, resale and their own feeds to the brand's store.
- **The numbers:** They will be 40% of luxury buyers by 2030 but are 10% of this brand's sales.
- **The catch:** What attracts them may put off today's loyal clients.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** Doubling the brand's sales to buyers under 30 without losing its older clients.

## What a strong answer addresses

- Who buyers aged 22 to 30 are and what they want (experiences, resale, their own feeds).
- The offer and channel that reach them without putting off today's loyal clients.
- How doubling sales to under-30s (10% of sales today, 40% of buyers by 2030) is measured, along with older clients kept.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
