# Expand to new clients without losing exclusivity

Luxury · Round 3 problem statement 21 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** A heritage watchmaker whose clients are mostly over 55.
- **The numbers:** Its clients spend Rs 12 lakh a year on average; it wins only 300 new ones a year.
- **The catch:** Every new client must feel as exclusive as the old ones; one mass campaign could undo 40 years of the brand.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** 1,000 new clients in a year with no fall in the average price paid.

## What a strong answer addresses

- Who the new clients are and how they are found and introduced without a mass campaign.
- How exclusivity is protected as the client base grows from 300 new clients a year to 1,000.
- How 'no fall in the average price paid' (about Rs 12 lakh a year per client) is measured.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
