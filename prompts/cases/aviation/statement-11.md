# Make 3 pilgrim routes break even within a year

Aviation · Round 3 problem statement 11 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** A state tourism department with three routes to temple towns.
- **The numbers:** The routes run at 41% seat load; each needs 60% to pay without subsidy, which ends in 12 months.
- **The catch:** Pilgrims travel in waves around festivals; the planes fly half-empty the rest of the year.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** All 3 routes average 60% load before the subsidy ends.

## What a strong answer addresses

- Where travellers come from outside the festival waves, and how seats are sold to them.
- The seat load month by month on each of the 3 routes, from 41% towards the 60% they need.
- What happens when the subsidy ends in 12 months.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
