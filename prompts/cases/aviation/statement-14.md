# Halve fog cancellations at a North-East airport

Aviation · Round 3 problem statement 14 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** A North-East airport that loses 60 days a year to fog and low cloud.
- **The numbers:** 1 flight in 4 is cancelled in winter; each cancellation costs the operator about ₹2 lakh and strands about 40 passengers.
- **The catch:** New landing aids cost crores and take years to approve.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** Weather cancellations halved within two winters.

## What a strong answer addresses

- What causes the cancellations (60 days a year of fog and low cloud; 1 flight in 4 cancelled in winter).
- How the solution deals with landing aids that cost crores and take years to approve.
- The cost against the saving (about ₹2 lakh and 40 stranded passengers per cancellation), and how halving within two winters is measured.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
