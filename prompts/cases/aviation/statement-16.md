# Get people from town to a runway 38 km away

Aviation · Round 3 problem statement 16 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** A bus operator serving the town around a small airport.
- **The numbers:** A taxi to the airport costs ₹1,200, more than a third of the average fare.
- **The catch:** With 4 flights a day, a bus that waits for every flight runs mostly empty.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** A feeder service that profits on every trip and gets travellers to the airport for under ₹300.

## What a strong answer addresses

- Routes and timings matched to 4 flights a day without a bus that runs mostly empty.
- The fare (under ₹300, against a ₹1,200 taxi) and how tickets are sold with the flight.
- The economics of each trip for the bus operator.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
