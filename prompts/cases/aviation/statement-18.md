# Export samples to buyers within 48 hours

Aviation · Round 3 problem statement 18 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** A brassware export cluster in a small town.
- **The numbers:** Samples take 7 days to reach buyers in the metros and abroad; 1 order in 5 is lost to the delay.
- **The catch:** Small parcels, export paperwork and a cargo hold that fills with luggage.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** 90% of samples with buyers within 48 hours.

## What a strong answer addresses

- The route from workshop to buyer, including small parcels and export paperwork.
- How space is secured when the hold fills with luggage.
- How the 48-hour target for 90% of samples is met and measured (7 days and 1 order in 5 lost today).
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.

## Fairness reminders

- Score substance, not length, jargon, polish or a confident tone. Give no credit for famous brand or people's names and no penalty for simple language or non-native English.
- Judge only what is written. Several different approaches can all score highly.
- The rubric decides the score; this brief only gives context.
