# Same-day delivery to 20 small towns

Aviation · Round 3 problem statement 17 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** An e-commerce company whose small-town orders take 5 days.
- **The numbers:** Each flight has up to 800 kg of cargo space, most of it unused.
- **The catch:** Space depends on how much luggage passengers carry, and the last stretch from the airport is by road.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** Same-day delivery on 20% of orders in 20 towns, at under ₹40 extra per order.

## What a strong answer addresses

- Which goods fly and how cargo space is booked when luggage varies (up to 800 kg a flight).
- The road link from airport to door in each town.
- The cost per order against under ₹40 extra, and how same-day delivery on 20% of orders in 20 towns is measured.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.

## Fairness reminders

- Score substance, not length, jargon, polish or a confident tone. Give no credit for famous brand or people's names and no penalty for simple language or non-native English.
- Judge only what is written. Several different approaches can all score highly.
- The rubric decides the score; this brief only gives context.
