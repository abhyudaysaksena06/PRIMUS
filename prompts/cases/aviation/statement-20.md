# A patient corridor to a metro cancer hospital

Aviation · Round 3 problem statement 20 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** A cancer hospital whose patients from 4 small towns travel 14 hours by road.
- **The numbers:** Patients need 6 to 10 visits; 1 in 5 misses treatment because of the journey.
- **The catch:** Patients are weak, usually travel with a family member, and cannot pay full fares.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** Missed treatments halved, with the trip under 4 hours door to door.

## What a strong answer addresses

- The journey door to door for weak patients with a family member, and how it fits under 4 hours (14 hours by road today).
- Who pays, since patients cannot pay full fares (hospital, state, insurer or other).
- How missed treatments (1 in 5 today, over 6 to 10 visits) are halved and measured.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
