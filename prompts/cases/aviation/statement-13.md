# Halve the days aircraft sit grounded

Aviation · Round 3 problem statement 13 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** A regional operator with 9 turboprops.
- **The numbers:** Each aircraft sits grounded about 30 days a year waiting for parts or engineers; each grounded day loses about ₹6 lakh.
- **The catch:** Spares come from abroad and engineers are based in the metros.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** Grounded days down to 15 a year per aircraft within 12 months.

## What a strong answer addresses

- Where the 30 grounded days a year come from (parts, engineers, paperwork).
- What changes, given that spares come from abroad and engineers are based in the metros.
- The saving at about ₹6 lakh a grounded day across 9 aircraft, and the path to 15 days a year within 12 months.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
