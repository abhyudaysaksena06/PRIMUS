# Make a 4-flight-a-day airport pay for itself

Aviation · Round 3 problem statement 15 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** The operator of a small airport that runs at a loss.
- **The numbers:** It costs ₹9 crore a year to run and earns ₹3 crore, almost all of it from flights.
- **The catch:** More flights depend on airlines, not on the airport.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** Income covers all costs within 3 years.

## What a strong answer addresses

- The new sources of income, given that more flights depend on airlines, not the airport.
- How each adds to the ₹3 crore earned against ₹9 crore of costs.
- The path year by year to covering all costs within 3 years.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.
