# Choose which 5 of 15 dormant airports to revive

Aviation · Round 3 problem statement 12 · judged as the company's solution (SOLUTION) or its hired firm's advice memo (ADVICE)

## The case in brief

- **Client:** A state government with 15 built airports that have no flights.
- **The numbers:** Reviving one costs about ₹12 crore over 3 years; the budget covers 5.
- **The catch:** Every district lobbies for its own airport, and past traffic forecasts were badly wrong.
- **Success, as the client defines it:** A choice the state can defend with data, and all 5 airports at 50% load within 2 years.

## What a strong answer addresses

- The data and method used to rank the 15 airports, given that past forecasts were badly wrong.
- How the choice is defended against district lobbying.
- The budget (about ₹12 crore each over 3 years, room for 5) and the path to 50% load within 2 years.
- Delivery (who does what, first step, cost) and a measure of success against the target, with a date.

## Common weak answers

- Generic advice ('use AI', 'build an app') with no mechanism, or restating the brief.
- Ignoring the catch, which is the main constraint.
- No numbers, no link from the plan to the target, or no measure of success.

## Fairness reminders

- Score substance, not length, jargon, polish or a confident tone. Give no credit for famous brand or people's names and no penalty for simple language or non-native English.
- Judge only what is written. Several different approaches can all score highly.
- The rubric decides the score; this brief only gives context.
